- SPACE / ENVIRONMENT: 56
- Scene Preset Hero: 48

## Asset policy

1. These are modular image assets, not screenshots of the whole UI.
2. Every master image must be independently reusable/repositionable.
3. UI_01 and UI_02 use the same semantic asset identity, with layout-specific crops/exports.
4. No competitor screenshots, logos, branded industrial-design copying, or redistributed IR/sample artwork.
5. SOURCE images use generic device archetypes.
6. TRANSMISSION images use abstract signal/waveform/link-quality metaphors rather than misleading device photos.
7. WALL/COVER images visually communicate obstruction/transmission.
8. SPACE images prioritize acoustic geometry/depth and scene readability.
9. Scene Hero images may combine source, path, obstruction and environment because they represent a complete preset.
10. Final asset approval requires visual consistency, readable crop at card size, and no accidental brand marks.

## Research basis

The catalog is intentionally broader than a single competitor. It is informed by recurring post-production categories such as phones, radios, televisions, walkies, PA/megaphones, speaker/device playback, covers/obstructions, indoor/outdoor spaces, vehicle interiors, transmission degradation, and worldizing workflows. The product uses independently designed generic categories and artwork.
