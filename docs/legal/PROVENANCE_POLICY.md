# SOURCERUNE Provenance / Licensing Policy

- Do not redistribute competitor screenshots, artwork, IRs, factory presets, source code, samples or proprietary measurements.
- Generic workflow ideas and public acoustic concepts may inform independent implementation.
- Every imported dependency, measurement, sample or visual asset needs an origin and license/provenance record before shipping.
- External IR/measurement data is R&D/calibration material unless redistribution rights are explicitly documented.
- Generated/internal artwork is reviewed for logos, copied trade dress and unintended resemblance.
